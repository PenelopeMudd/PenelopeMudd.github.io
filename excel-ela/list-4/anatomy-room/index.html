Mission briefing

The lab needs your help.

Nobody is hurt—this is a vocabulary training lab. Your job is to connect medical word parts with their meanings to unlock the building.

Your toolsKeep Vocabulary List 4 nearby. You may use it, ask for a hint, and try again as many times as you need. There's no timer and no penalty for mistakes.

Three lab stations will each give you one number. You will match six word parts to pictures, type missing medical word parts, and enter the final door code.
